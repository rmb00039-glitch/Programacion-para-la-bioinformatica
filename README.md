#### **This repositories verse about the two projects done during "Programación para la Bioinformática" Lecture, encompassed in the Bioinformatics and Biostatistics Master taught by the University of Barcelona and the Open University of Cantalunya**
##### The first and second assignments (PECs) focus on learning the Python language and its syntax.
##### The third covers the use of libraries such as NumPy and Matplotlib. 
##### The fourth focuses on the use of biological libraries in Python.
##### And the fifth on the implementation of tests.
